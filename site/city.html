Race rules and skill rewards

Power-ups: drive through a mystery box, press L to use your item or X to discard it. Each box returns after 10 seconds for everyone. Turbo, bomb, shield, oil, grip and nitro recharge are available in Arcade. Each of the six items has an equal chance in multiplayer; solo awards the three self-use items equally.

Skill credits: competitive clean laps, clean technical sectors, personal bests, sustained overtakes and defences can earn credits. Successful attacks, blocks and evasions also count in Arcade. Three SC become one REP, with at most three SC per lap and one combat SC per lap.

Engineering: stop in the marked box midway along the pit lane and press P. Spend up to two REP per lap to move performance between attributes, keeping the total at 14. The race keeps running. Pro uses pre-race tuning only.

Slipstream: follow a nearby car closely for one second to gain a short speed advantage. It does not stack with nitro or turbo.
